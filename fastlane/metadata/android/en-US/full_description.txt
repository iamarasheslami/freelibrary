FreeLibrary is a fast, private reader for public-domain books. Browse a built-in catalog of about 78,000 titles, download the ones you want and read them offline.

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li>Search the full catalog by title, author, language and genre</li>
<li>Download books in the formats you prefer and read them in the built-in reader</li>
<li>Shelves for saved, currently-reading and finished books, with reading progress and bookmarks</li>
<li>Light, dark and system themes</li>
<li>Back up and restore your library to a file you control</li>
</ul>

<b>Private by design</b>
<ul>
<li>No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking</li>
<li>Only two permissions: internet and network state</li>
<li>Your shelves and progress never leave your device</li>
</ul>

FreeLibrary is free software released under the GNU GPLv3.
